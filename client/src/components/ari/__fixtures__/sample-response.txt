## Empiric coverage recommendation

For suspected hospital-acquired pneumonia in a UCLA Health ICU patient, start dual anti-pseudomonal coverage:

- **Cefepime** 2 g IV q8h
- **Tobramycin** 7 mg/kg IV q24h
- **Vancomycin** 25–30 mg/kg load if MRSA risk factors are present (recent IV antibiotics, prior MRSA colonization, or local MRSA prevalence >20%)
