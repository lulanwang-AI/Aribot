---
name: Aribots product routing
description: Product boundaries and hub relationship across ARIbots, Ari AMR, and Ari EMR.
---

Use the ARIbots V4 public site as the shared Ari platform hub, with clear connections to three distinct applications. Present the public story as one connected clinical system: ARIbots for clinical intelligence and decision support, Ari AMR for antimicrobial stewardship, and Ari EMR for records and care operations. ARIbots keeps its own clinician login and authenticated community/assistant experience. Ari AMR keeps its own product page, login, and antimicrobial stewardship workspace. Ari EMR remains a standalone application with its own server and clinical data model.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants one website that connects all three applications while preserving each product’s routes, login boundaries, purpose, and application shell.

Public messaging should address multidisciplinary care teams, stewardship leaders, healthcare organizations, and health systems rather than positioning Ari as only a physician community. Public AMR and EMR pages should feel like members of the ARIbots visual family, while each authenticated workspace keeps its domain-specific clinical tokens and information architecture.

**How to apply:** When changing navigation, authentication, landing pages, APIs, or app shells, verify all three product paths independently. Do not share redirects or API paths in ways that move users or data into another product. Apply shared branding at the public-page layer rather than globally restyling the clinical applications.
